AudioBridge

Plays your Mac's audio on your Android phone, over USB or Wi-Fi.

This installs AudioBridge.app into your Applications folder. It runs as a normal app with a window, and also puts a waveform icon in your menu bar.

After installing: press Start, then scan the QR code with the AudioBridge app on your phone.

macOS will ask for one permission the first time you start it: Screen & System Audio Recording for the default capture method, or the microphone if you switch to BlackHole. Either is used only to read the audio — no image is ever captured.
